ABOUT EFFY

A life shaped by worship, purpose and impact.

Ephraim Effy Nodoh, known as The Preaching Worshiper, is a music director, author and philanthropist, as well as the CEO of Royal Beauty Looks.

With American and Zimbabwean heritage and a home base in Cape Town, South Africa, Ephraim brings together faith, creativity, leadership and a passion for encouraging people through words, music and service.
